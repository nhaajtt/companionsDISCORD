Nine personalities. Zero dead channels.

Small bots with thirty different personalities that keep a quiet Discord channel from going quiet: questions, jokes, fun facts, polls, trivia rounds with a leaderboard, and the occasional argument about pineapple on pizza.

If nobody answers, another bot jumps in. If a person joins, the bots step back and even say thank you.

  • 2 to 10 bots
  • trivia, polls, question of the day
  • sits in voice 24/7
  • always wear the BOT tag
  • never read message content
  • no outside services

Personality is picked by the order of the tokens: the first token is Pip, the second is Grumble, and so on. The ninth is Cow, then Rex (movie-trailer voice), Maple (cozy adviser), Rocket (countdown optimist), Zip (always in a hurry), Clue (noir detective), Mochi (gentle encourager), Waffle (dumb puns), Tofu (deadpan, very literal), Ziggy (hype DJ), Echo (repeats the last words), Misty (vague prophecies), Byte (glitchy robot), furyZ (Valorant gamer), Chamy (furyZ's girlfriend), Sizzle (cooking-show chef), Gizmo (inventor), Anchor (sea captain), Jinx (fortune-cookie omens), Quill (pompous professor), Blip (anxious worrier) and Sparky (power puns). With fewer bots the first ones are used, and a 31st bot would share Pip's personality (the program uses at most 30 tokens). Every personality has its own lines and 25 notes in both languages. Fun facts come from a bank of about 1,250, checked and filtered: outdated or disputed claims were left out (we still would not bet your pub quiz on it).

S-02

How a conversation goes

Four steps
usually over in a few minutes

  1. One bot starts

    A question, a joke or riddle, a fun fact, a line of banter, a poll or a trivia round.

  2. A person answers, the bots step back

    They stay out of the way (a joke's punchline is still delivered), and whoever replied to a bot gets a short thank-you after a natural pause.

  3. Nobody answers for several minutes

    Another bot answers or shrugs, a third may react, and the teller of a joke gives the punchline.

  4. Then it goes quiet for a good while

    Normally 1.5 to 4 hours. Chatty, not needy.

S-03

Playing, not just talking

Six ways to get people involved
all switchable per server

Question of the day

One question at a fixed hour, every day, set with /companions qotd [hour]. It is posted even during quiet hours, because some traditions outrank bedtime.

Polls

Real Discord polls. A few minutes later, another bot reacts to the result, whether it was asked to or not.

Trivia rounds

Four answer buttons, A to D. Your first press counts and is locked in privately, so nobody can copy you. After 8 to 15 minutes another bot reveals the answer, lists who got it right (without pinging them) and turns the buttons off.

Leaderboard

Everyone who got it right earns a point. /trivia top shows the weekly or the all-time board, and /trivia forget lets anyone erase their own scores.

Stats

/companions stats [days] shows how many conversations got people talking or playing, per kind. Handy for finding out whether the bots are charming or merely loud.

Your own content

Managers can add their own questions, jokes, facts and polls with /companions content, no redeploy needed. See the setup page.

There are about 300 trivia questions in each language. They are built from the fun-fact bank, so every answer is backed by a fact in the repo. Trivia and polls can each be switched off with /companions toggle.

S-05

Sitting in voice 24/7

A voice room that looks lived in
even while everyone sleeps

They stay put

Type /companions voice join channel:#voice-room bots:2, then add more rooms with the same command: each bot keeps its room, and a new or bigger room only uses bots that are free (lowest numbers first), never ones from another room. Remove a room and its bots become free, the other rooms stay as they are. The bots join (no mute or deafen icon), and keep sitting there even after everyone has left. After a restart or a dropped connection they rejoin on their own, waiting longer and longer if Discord is having a day.

No talking, no listening

They just sit. They play no audio, record nothing and receive no audio. All they need is the View Channel and Connect permissions.

A greeting when you walk in

Turn on /companions voice greet and a bot says hello in the text channel when someone joins the room (at least 30 minutes apart per person, and never during quiet hours).

Pomodoro focus sessions

/pomodoro start while you are in the room: the bots announce the start, the break and the end. If the room stays empty for 5 minutes, the session stops by itself.

Voice time leaderboard

/voice top ranks who has sat in the room the longest, this week or all time. /voice forget erases your time.

S-06

Useful for the community

Welcome newcomers, nudge people
count down to events

Welcome new members

/companions welcome: when someone joins the server, a bot greets them with a joke and an icebreaker to get a conversation going. It uses Discord's own system messages, so no special permission is needed.

Reminders

/remind add in:2h text:... and a bot reminds you on time in the channel, using your name. Up to 20 reminders, and each one is deleted as soon as it is delivered.

Event countdowns

/event add in:3d name:... (for managers): the bots announce it a day before, an hour before, and when it starts.

A weekly recap

/companions recap: on Monday morning the bots post last week's conversations, the trivia champion and who spent the most time in voice.

Streaks and regulars

Answer trivia right on consecutive days and the bots cheer at 3, 7, 14 and 30 days (/trivia streak). Five hours in the voice room in a week earns a voice regular shout-out. No roles are changed.

Seasonal

Halloween, Christmas, New Year, Tet and Valentine have their own questions, jokes, facts and polls, switched on by date in both languages.

It learns what works

Kinds of conversation that get your members talking are picked more often (up to twice as often). /companions stats also shows the best hour.

It looks alive

Each bot shows a status under its name: sitting in voice with a few people, minutes left in a focus session, or a funny idle line.

Safe to leave alone

Webhook alerts when a bot drops, a daily backup of the data, export and delete your server's data with /companions data, and the data erases itself a day after every bot has left.

Check if the bots are okay

The status page: how many bots are online, how many voice rooms have bots sitting in them, how many conversations happened last week. Totals only, no names and no messages.

S-07

Good manners

Cute has limits
and all of them are adjustable

  • One channelThey talk only in the channel you pick. No wandering.
  • Quiet hours23:00 to 08:00 by default, in your time zone. Even bots need their beauty sleep.
  • A daily limitCalm up to 4 conversations a day, Normal 8, Lively 14. They will not spam you.
  • Nothing while people chatIf humans are talking, the bots sit on their hands.
  • Typing indicatorsThey show "is typing" like anyone polite would.
  • No pingsNo @everyone, no @anybody. Thank-yous are gentle, with a ten minute cooldown between them.
  • No message contentThey only need to know that someone wrote or replied, never what was written.
S-08

The honest bit

Tested on paper
not yet field-tested

not yet live

The conversation logic is covered by automated tests with a fake clock and fake bots (npm test). It has not been run on a live Discord server yet, so expect to tune the timing and the content after watching it for a few days. Use it in servers you run, tell your members what these bots are, and keep the frequency modest.
