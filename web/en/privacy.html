Privacy

Short, because the bots have almost nothing to hide: a few settings and counters, trivia scores, no message reading, no outside services.

Last updated: 2026-10-02rev II

What the bots store

Everything is kept in data/, on the machine that runs the bots:

  • companions.json: per-server settings (channel, language, frequency, quiet hours, question of the day, trivia and poll switches).
  • usage.json: daily counters only, meaning how many conversations of each kind were started and engaged. No messages and no user ids.
  • custom.json: the questions, jokes, facts and polls your managers added.
  • scores.json: the Discord user ids of people who answered a trivia round correctly, with their points per week.
  • voice.json: minutes people spent in the voice room the companions sit in: user ids with minutes per week. /voice forget erases a person's entries.
  • reminders.json: waiting reminders and event countdowns, meaning the text typed and the author's user id. Each entry is deleted once it is delivered or cancelled.
  • backups/: a copy of the files above, taken once a day. The newest 7 days are kept, so an erased person's entries also disappear from the backups within a week.

User ids are stored in scores.json, voice.json and reminders.json and nowhere else. Each person can erase their own scores with /trivia forget and their voice time with /voice forget. Nothing else about a person is stored, and no message is ever stored.

What the bots do not do

  • They do not read message content. The only intents they use are Guilds, GuildMessages and GuildVoiceStates: no message content and no members intent. They only notice that someone wrote, replied or joined the voice room.
  • They never speak, play or listen in voice. They just sit in the room, with no mute or deafen icon, and receive no audio.
  • They send nothing to outside services, except a short health message (a bot is offline, a voice join keeps failing) to a webhook that you set yourself, which never contains member data. The banks of questions, jokes and fun facts (about 1,250 fun facts and about 300 trivia questions in each language, checked and filtered) are written into the repo.
  • They do not pretend to be human: the bots keep Discord's BOT tag.

Self-hosted means you are responsible

This project runs on your own machine. The data lives there, you manage it, and you are responsible for your own server. Tell your members what these bots are, and keep the frequency modest.

Third parties

Discord processes messages and commands under Discord's own policies. This website is static, has no tracking, and only remembers your light or dark theme choice in your own browser.

Deleting it

Anyone can erase their own trivia scores with /trivia forget and their voice time with /voice forget. To remove everything, delete the files in data/ (companions.json, usage.json, custom.json, scores.json, voice.json, reminders.json) on the machine that runs the bots, or remove the bots from your server. That is the whole procedure.
