Privacy

Short, because the bots have almost nothing to hide: a few settings, no message reading, no outside services.

Last updated: 2026-10-02rev I

What the bots store

  • Per-server settings: the channel, language, frequency and quiet hours.
  • They live in data/companions.json, on the machine that runs the bots.
  • That is all: no messages, no user data.

What the bots do not do

  • They do not read message content. They only use the non-privileged Guilds and GuildMessages intents, to notice that someone wrote or replied.
  • They send nothing to outside services. The banks of questions, jokes and fun facts are written into the repo.
  • They do not pretend to be human: the bots keep Discord's BOT tag.

Self-hosted means you are responsible

This project runs on your own machine. The data lives there, you manage it, and you are responsible for your own server. Tell your members what these bots are, and keep the frequency modest.

Third parties

Discord processes messages and commands under Discord's own policies. This website is static, has no tracking, and only remembers your light or dark theme choice in your own browser.

Deleting it

Delete data/companions.json on the machine that runs the bots, or remove the bots from your server. That is the whole procedure.
